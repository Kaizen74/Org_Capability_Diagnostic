Four apps, one workflow

How to sequence Capability Compass, Org Modeling, Process Map Studio and the AI Org Advisory Workbench to assess and design a fit-for-purpose operating model and system. Start with the current-state analysis; the rest of the journey follows from it.

Your immediate need. Nine steps that frame the analysis, gather the evidence in each country, and end with six pages for EXCO. Click a step for exactly where to go and what "done" looks like. Ticks are remembered on this computer.

The whole journey: six stages, four apps. Each card is what one app does in one stage. Click a card for the question it answers and what it passes on. Empty cells mean that app has no part in that stage.

What travels between the apps. Two small files and one shared language. Nothing is retyped, and every ID means the same thing in every app and in every country.

Why a difference between countries exists: three words, used in every app

Required

Regulation, a customer contract or the physical site makes it so.→ Leave local

Chosen

A deliberate local decision, made for a reason someone can state.→ Decide: keep or align

Inherited

History, an acquisition or habit. Nobody decided it.→ Harmonise

Each country has its own key in front (for example a frame key plus a two-letter country code), so an ID quoted in a board paper always says which country it came from.

What EXCO sees. The current-state analysis ends in six pages, printed from Compass → Current State → EXCO readout. You write two things: the verdict and the ask. Everything else is counted or quoted, so every line traces to a score or a finding.

Before you present

  • Every country fully scored. An unscored cell reads "Not scored"; it is never counted as weak.
  • Every material difference (two levels or more) classed Required, Chosen or Inherited, with a one-line reason.
  • Findings imported for each country, or pages 3 to 5 will say plainly that they are empty.
  • Country leads have looked at each other's evidence for the widest gaps. Different people score different countries, and one may simply be stricter.
  • Money at risk is an estimate until finance validates it, and is only totalled when the countries share a currency.
