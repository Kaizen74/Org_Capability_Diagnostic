Capability Compass briefing

Methodology: 4-level maturity scale (Absent → Early → Emerging → Established) on a colorblind-safe navy ramp; amber flags mark Level 1–2 cells. Trajectory = version-over-version movement blended 50/50 with user-maintained leading indicators; projections span two review cycles and are directional, not deterministic. Every rating should cite evidence — uncited ratings are flagged “Unsupported”. All data is stored only in this browser (localStorage); use Export for backups.
